## Introduction

A paid [MUFON]({{ 'mufon/' | relative_url }}) membership provides substantially more access than the organisation's public-facing website, but it does not unlock the organisation's entire investigative archive. Members can search large portions of the Case Management System (CMS), read witness narratives, and, depending on the membership level and the material available for a case, view attachments such as photographs, videos and uploaded documents. At the same time, MUFON maintains additional layers of restricted information for privacy, case management and investigative purposes. Witness contact details, internal investigator notes, administrative records and other sensitive material are not presented as part of ordinary member access. This layered approach is intended to support civilian UFO research while protecting witnesses and preserving the integrity of ongoing investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mufon.com/search_database-terms-and-conditions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search Database Terms and Conditions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Database Terms and Conditions - MUFON...</span></span></span>

## Reports and Attachments Available to Members

MUFON describes database access as a membership privilege rather than a public archive. Its published database terms state that active members may access "certain parts" of the Case Management System, specifically including sighting narratives together with uploaded photographs, videos, Microsoft Word documents and PDF files. The wording is notable because it explicitly refers to selected portions of the database rather than unrestricted access to every record held by the organisation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mufon.com/search_database-terms-and-conditions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search Database Terms and Conditions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Database Terms and Conditions - MUFON...</span></span></span>

## What Members Do Not See

Membership should not be confused with investigator or administrator privileges. MUFON's own documentation consistently distinguishes between member access and confidential case-management information. Although members can search many reports, several important categories of information remain restricted.

These typically include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* witness names, addresses, telephone numbers and other personal identifiers;
* investigator correspondence with witnesses;<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://mufonu.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufonu.com">[mufonu.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufonu.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MUFON Field Investigators are the heart and soul of our organization. * internal case-management notes;
* administrative workflow records;
* information used to assign or supervise investigations;
* material withheld to protect privacy or ongoing investigations.

</div>

This distinction reflects MUFON's dual role. The organisation functions both as a public reporting service and as an investigative network. Witnesses often provide personal details with the expectation that investigators—not the general membership—will use them to conduct interviews or request clarification. Restricting that information reduces the risk of unwanted contact and helps preserve witness confidentiality.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mufon.com/search_database-terms-and-conditions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search Database Terms and Conditions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Database Terms and Conditions - MUFON...</span></span></span>

The arrangement also means that even paying members cannot independently audit every stage of an investigation. They may see the submitted evidence available through the member database while remaining unable to inspect internal discussions, investigator deliberations or administrative decisions that contributed to a case's eventual classification.

## Why MUFON Uses Layered Access

The membership model balances several competing objectives.

First, it supports research by allowing paying members to search a large archive of civilian UFO reports rather than limiting access to isolated published cases.

Secondly, it protects witnesses. Many reports contain sensitive personal information or circumstances that witnesses may not wish to become publicly searchable. Restricting identifying information helps encourage reporting while reducing privacy risks.

Finally, it supports case management. Investigators need working documents, interview notes and administrative tools that are not intended as public research material. Separating operational records from member-facing records reduces the likelihood that incomplete investigations, draft analyses or internal communications will be mistaken for final conclusions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mufon.com/search_database-terms-and-conditions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search Database Terms and Conditions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Database Terms and Conditions - MUFON...</span></span></span>

## Copyright and Limits on Redistribution

Access to the database does not transfer ownership of its contents. MUFON's published terms state that reports and media remain protected by United States copyright, with ownership retained by MUFON and the original submitting party where applicable. Members are granted permission to access the material through their membership, not to republish it freely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://mufon.com/search_database-terms-and-conditions/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: mufon.com">[MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">mufon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Search Database Terms and Conditions</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Search Database Terms and Conditions - MUFON...</span></span></span>

The organisation specifically prohibits downloading database content for redistribution through books, magazines, television programmes, films, websites, social media or other commercial or non-commercial platforms without prior written permission.
